Question 41: Is a missing value the same as zero or an empty string?
Answer: No. Missing, zero, and empty are distinct states unless the schema explicitly defines them as equivalent.

Question 42: What is the purpose of integrity rules?
Answer: To prevent representable states that violate the dataset's declared semantics.

Question 43: What is a functional dependency X → Y?
Answer: Whenever two rows agree on X, they must also agree on Y; X determines Y.

Question 44: What is data redundancy?
Answer: The same logical fact is stored in multiple places.

Question 45: What is normalization?
Answer: Lossless decomposition of relations into simpler relations to reduce redundancy and inconsistency.

Question 46: What is an update anomaly?
Answer: A contradiction caused when one logical fact is duplicated and only some copies are updated.

Question 47: What is the core normalization principle?
Answer: Store independent facts according to their dependencies so each logical fact has a clear authoritative location.

Question 48: Is denormalization always wrong?
Answer: No. It can be chosen deliberately for performance, but duplicates facts and therefore requires consistency control.

Question 49: What is structural metadata?
Answer: Metadata describing data structure, such as format, syntax, or semantics.

Question 50: What is descriptive metadata?
Answer: Metadata describing data contents or characteristics.
